# 🚀 CareerPilot AI

### An AI-Powered Personalized Career Guidance & Skill Gap Analysis Platform

CareerPilot AI is an AI-powered career guidance and placement-readiness platform designed to help students bridge the gap between their current skills and industry requirements.

Instead of simply telling students whether they are qualified for a job, CareerPilot AI analyzes their current profile against their target role and answers:

> **What am I missing, and what should I do next?**

---

## 🎯 Problem

Students preparing for internships and placements often struggle to understand the gap between their current profile and the requirements of their target roles.

Common challenges include:

- Lack of personalized career guidance
- Difficulty understanding Job Description (JD) requirements
- Resume–JD mismatch
- Unclear skill gaps
- Random or inefficient learning
- Lack of role-specific project recommendations
- Scattered interview preparation resources
- Limited visibility into personal placement readiness

Students often know **where they want to go**, but not **how to get there**.

---

## 💡 Solution

CareerPilot AI creates a personalized career pathway using a student's:

- 📄 Resume
- 💻 GitHub profile
- 🎯 Target job role
- 🏢 Target company
- 📋 Job description

The platform analyzes the student's profile against industry requirements and identifies:

- ✅ Matched skills
- ⚠️ Partially developed skills
- ❌ Missing skills

It then generates personalized recommendations for:

- Learning paths
- Projects
- Certifications
- Resume improvements
- Interview preparation
- Skill development

The system also tracks progress and continuously updates the student's career-readiness profile.
